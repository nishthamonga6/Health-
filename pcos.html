Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS)

Written by Health Team | Reviewed by Dr. Kavita Nanda | Updated: Apr 2025
PCOS is a common hormonal disorder affecting 1 in 10 women. It leads to irregular periods, hormonal imbalance, acne, weight gain, and can affect fertility. Awareness and lifestyle changes can help manage it effectively.

Symptoms of PCOS

  • Irregular or missed periods
  • Excess facial or body hair (hirsutism)
  • Acne or oily skin
  • Weight gain or difficulty losing weight
  • Thinning hair on the scalp
  • Fertility problems or difficulty conceiving
  • Darkening of skin, especially on neck or groin

Causes of PCOS

  • Excess insulin production leading to hormone imbalance
  • Higher levels of androgens (male hormones)
  • Low-grade inflammation in the body
  • Genetics and family history

Treatment & Management

  • Lifestyle: Regular exercise, healthy low-GI diet, weight management
  • Medical: Hormonal birth control, Metformin, fertility treatment if needed
  • Self-care: Stress management, sleep hygiene, symptom tracking

When to Consult a Doctor

  • Missed periods for 2+ months
  • Fertility concerns or trying to conceive
  • Severe acne or hair growth
  • Unexplained weight gain or mental health issues
