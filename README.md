HealthMonitor is a web application designed to help users track their menstrual cycles, access health information, and book doctor appointments. With a user-friendly interface, it empowers individuals to take charge of their health and well-being.
# 💜 GenZ Health — Talk to a Gyno 👩‍⚕️🌸

A vibrant, professional, and Gen Z-styled frontend feature that lets users **search, filter, and review gynecologists** with style! Includes adorable UI, interactive filters, star ratings, and user-submitted reviews to make healthcare approachable and intuitive.

## 🚀 Features

- 🔍 **Live Search:** Search by name, experience, or expertise.
- 🎯 **Interactive Filters:** Online only, PCOS specialist, PMS, teen-friendly, and more.
- 🌟 **Ratings & Reviews:** Users can rate (1–5 stars) and write short reviews for each gynecologist.
- 💬 **Real-time Online Status** indicators.
- 💖 **Cute, Accessible & Gen Z-friendly UI** with pastel gradients, animations, and emoji enhancements.


---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML, TailwindCSS**
- **JavaScript (Vanilla)**
- **Responsive Design**
