🌸 Common Women’s Health Issues

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS)

A hormonal imbalance that causes irregular periods, weight gain, acne, and excessive hair growth. Can affect fertility.

Full Details

Endometriosis

A painful condition where uterine-like tissue grows outside the uterus, leading to pain, cramping, and infertility.

Urinary Tract Infections (UTIs)

Causes include bacteria entering the urinary tract. Symptoms are pain while urinating, urgency, and cloudy urine.

Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS)

Mood swings, bloating, and fatigue before periods. Often managed with exercise, hydration, and a healthy diet.

Vaginal Infections

Bacterial or yeast infections cause itching, discharge, and irritation. Treatment depends on the type of infection.

Breast Cancer

Cancer that develops in breast tissue. Early signs include lumps, changes in shape, or discharge. Early detection is key.

🛍️ Products to Use During Your Period

🩲 Sanitary Pads

Best for beginners! Easy to use, comfortable, and available everywhere.

Pads stick to your underwear and absorb flow. Change every 4–6 hours. They come in thin (pantyliners) to thick (overnight) types. Great for heavy days and newbies!

🧵 Tampons

Small, discreet, and inserted inside. Good for active days or swimming!

Tampons go inside the vaginal canal and absorb blood. Always wash your hands, and never keep them in for more than 8 hours to avoid health risks (like TSS). Available with or without applicators.

🥣 Menstrual Cups

Eco-friendly & reusable. Inserted like a tampon, but it collects instead of absorbs.

Made of medical-grade silicone, cups hold your flow for up to 12 hours. Fold, insert, and go! Rinse and reuse. Perfect if you're eco-conscious and don’t mind a learning curve.

🩳 Period Underwear

Leak-proof undies that absorb blood. Super comfy & reusable!

Looks like regular underwear but has super-absorbent layers built in. Wear alone or as backup. Rinse after use, machine wash, air dry. Great for school days or sleep.

🩱 Panty Liners

Perfect for light spotting or daily freshness.

Liners are super thin pads for light flow or discharge. Use them at the beginning or end of your period, or daily if you like extra protection. Just change every few hours!

🔥 Heating Patches

Period cramps? These stick-on patches soothe pain and keep you moving!

Stick a patch on your belly or back — it warms up and relaxes muscles. You can wear them under clothes, at school, or even while sleeping. No pills, just cozy relief!
